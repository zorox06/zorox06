```yaml

  - Machine Learning
  - Data Science
  - Quantitative Finance
  - FinTech Analytics
  - Full-Stack AI Products
currently_building:
  - ML-ready analytics dashboards
  - Finance and tax automation tools
  - Prediction-market and data-driven decision systems
```

I build at the intersection of **machine learning, data science, finance, and product engineering**.

My sweet spot is taking messy real-world data, cleaning it, modeling it, explaining it, and shipping it inside a usable product. I care about the full loop: **data pipelines, feature engineering, model evaluation, dashboards, APIs, and deployment**.

- Currently sharpening my depth in **Python, statistics, machine learning, SQL, and system design**
- Interested in **quant research, risk modeling, market prediction, portfolio analytics, and financial automation**
- Comfortable moving across the stack with **Python, TypeScript, React, PostgreSQL, and modern web tooling**
- Open to roles in **Data Science, Machine Learning Engineering, Quant/FinTech Analytics, and AI Product Engineering**

> Recruiter TL;DR: I do not just train models or just build UIs. I connect the data, model, product, and business problem until the thing actually works.
